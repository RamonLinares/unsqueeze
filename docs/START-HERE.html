UNSQUEEZE / START HERE

Your lens. Natural proportions.

Correct anamorphic camera video in Google Meet. Other people in your call receive the corrected image.

You need desktop Google Chrome and a camera that already works in Meet. No terminal, account, or build tools are needed.

  1. Keep this folder somewhere permanent

    Unzip the download first. Move the Unsqueeze folder to Documents or another permanent location. It must contain manifest.json and this guide. Keep the folder there while the extension is installed.

  2. Open Chrome’s extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions into Chrome’s address bar and press Enter. Turn on Developer mode at the top right.

  3. Load the Unsqueeze folder

    Click Load unpacked and select the folder containing manifest.json. Select the whole folder, not the ZIP or an individual file. An Unsqueeze — Anamorphic Webcam card should appear.

  4. Pin it and choose your lens factor

    Click Chrome’s puzzle-piece Extensions icon, then the pin beside Unsqueeze. Open Unsqueeze, leave Desqueeze video on, and select your lens’s squeeze factor. Use a preset or type a custom value from 1× to 3×.

    Use the lens’s squeeze ratio, not its focal length. A 1.5× lens needs 1.5×. A regular lens needs 1× or correction off. Start with Efficient quality; use Low power to reduce processing load.

  5. Reload Meet and use your normal camera

    Reload any open Google Meet tabs. In Meet → Settings → Video, select your normal camera. Unsqueeze corrects that feed; there is no additional virtual camera to select.

    After enabling correction, changing the camera filter, or changing capture quality, turn Meet’s camera off and back on.

Check the result before a call

Open Unsqueeze → Open camera preview → Test pattern. Set the factor to 1.5×: the circle should be round. This test uses no camera. You can also choose Start camera to check your real image. Click Stop before using your camera in Meet.

Update later

Download the latest release and replace the files inside this same folder, keeping its location. Open chrome://extensions, click Reload on Unsqueeze, then reload Meet. Updates are manual. Avoid installing a second copy.

Need a hand?

If Chrome reports a missing manifest, select the folder directly containing manifest.json. If the picture is still squeezed, reload Meet and check the factor and camera filter. Work or school browsers may restrict manual installation.
